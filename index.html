About

More than a skillset and engineering knowledge.

Aerospace started with my uncle, an aerospace engineer. When I was a kid he showed me videos and pictures from his workplace, and it looked like something out of a movie. It's been the plan ever since.

At TMU I've designed a retractable overhead-bin ladder for the Airbus A380, reverse-engineered and redesigned a combat-robot gearbox, built a six-degree-of-freedom flight model I can fly with a joystick, analyzed linkages for a walking robot, and helped launch and recover a competition rocket with the Metropolitan Aerospace Rocket Society. On my own time I taught myself Swift and SwiftUI to build Optimal, an all-in-one planner app for iPhone that's now in public beta.

A lot of who I am also comes from outside the classroom. I lead outreach for a funeral aid nonprofit, spent a summer in Bosnia with Islamic Relief, started a debate program from nothing, was head coach at a swim school, and for the past three years at Techspan I've been the one getting sales, technical and web teams to agree on how 30,000+ products should be listed. I've worked in a lot of different fields with a lot of different people, and that gives me a different perspective on an engineering team.

I bring both sides of the spectrum.
